Privacy Policy

Processing of personal data on the svkatto.fi website

This policy explains how Svkatto Oy processes personal data on the svkatto.fi website in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Updated 8/2026.

1. Data controller

Svkatto Oy (Business ID 3510360-8)
Pähkinätie 12 A1, 01710 Vantaa, Finland
Email: info@svkatto.fi · Phone: 050 581 3227

2. What data we process and its source

  • Contact form: name, email address and message content that you provide.
  • Technical log data: information automatically collected by the web server, such as IP address and browser details.

3. Purpose and legal basis

  • Responding to contact and quote requests. Legal basis: legitimate interest and your consent.
  • Technical operation and security of the website. Legal basis: legitimate interest.

4. Recipients and processors

We use trusted service providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Zoner Oy – website hosting and server (Finland/EU).
  • Formspree, Inc. – delivery of contact form messages (USA).
  • Google (Google Fonts) – delivery of website fonts; transmits the visitor's IP address.
  • OpenStreetMap – map images for the map feature.

We do not sell or disclose personal data for marketing purposes.

5. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Formspree, which delivers the contact form, operates in the USA, so data you send via the form may be transferred outside the EU/EEA. The transfer is based on appropriate safeguards (including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

6. Cookies

The website does not use tracking or marketing cookies or any analytics tools. We do not place cookies on the visitor's device.

7. Retention period

We retain contact messages only as long as necessary to handle the matter, after which they are deleted. Technical log data is retained for the period defined by the service provider.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, request its correction or erasure, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw your consent. You may also lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi). To exercise your rights, contact the address above.

9. Contact

For data protection matters: info@svkatto.fi.
