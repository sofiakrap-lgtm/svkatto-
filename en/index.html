Company

Roofs done right
the first time

We came into the roofing trade by chance, but years of work and experience gained from our employers gave us solid professional skills and practical know-how. Over time our goals and our view of the work changed, and we realised we were ready to build something of our own.

With our own company we gained the chance to work more independently and to offer clients our own solutions, finding the best option for every project. It is important to us that the work is done to a high standard, thoughtfully and with the client's needs in mind. Our operations are insured (Pohjola Vakuutus) and we are a Reliable Partner (Luotettava Kumppani).
