Svkatto Oy - Services

Interior Renovations

Renovation of interior spaces

We carry out renovation and modification works in the communal areas of properties, such as stairwells and basement spaces. We aim to minimise disruption to residents and notify them in advance.

Skilled operations

In renovation projects, works are carried out according to the client's brief or an existing specification. We have a skilled workforce with broad expertise, as well as partners for all trades required in renovation construction.

Larger interior contracts

We primarily undertake larger interior renovation contracts throughout the Helsinki metropolitan area. We serve mainly housing companies and major property owners.
