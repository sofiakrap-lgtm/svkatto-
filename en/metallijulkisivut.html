Svkatto Oy - Services

Facade & Balcony Renovations

Building renovation in the Helsinki metropolitan area

We carry out repair and renewal works on facades and balconies as the responsible main contractor, in accordance with the client's needs and plans. We operate primarily in the Helsinki metropolitan area. Our reference projects demonstrate the wide-ranging expertise and professionalism of our staff.

Facade Renovation

With a skilled contractor, a property's facade can be made as good as new. We carry out facade renovations comprehensively, from rendering to timber cladding, and from window and exterior door replacements to painting. We undertake all types of rendering work, from marmorino to thermal render. We also renew facade decorations, from plaster ornaments to gilding.

Balcony Renovation

Balcony renovations, particularly for concrete structures, require specialist expertise. We have been carrying out balcony renovations alongside facade works for approximately 30 years, so our know-how is well established. Balcony renovations, like all other repairs, are built on thorough condition surveys and careful planning.
