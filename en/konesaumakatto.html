Sheet Metal Roof Repairs

Roof renovations in the Helsinki metropolitan area

We renew various types of sheet metal roofs, which may range from simple lean-to roofs to complex early 20th-century apartment building roofs. Materials used include galvanised, copper or stainless steel sheet metal. Where required, we renew the roof's rainwater systems and roof safety products.

The roof is a whole

A roof is not merely a visible surface, but an essential part of the property's overall condition. When carrying out maintenance works on a roof, the survey must go beyond the surface to the underlying structure, so that all potential problems can be identified and addressed. We carry out roof renewals thoroughly.

A long-lasting result

A professionally installed sheet metal roof is an investment that stands the test of time. With regular inspections and maintenance, the roof's service life is extended by several years.
