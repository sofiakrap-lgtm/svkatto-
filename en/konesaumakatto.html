Svkatto Oy, Services

Standing seam and lock seam roofs

Installation, repair and renewal of standing seam and lock seam roofs in Uusimaa

We install, repair and renew standing seam and lock seam roofs with professional skill. We replace old metal roofs with durable ones and carry out the work taking each site's particular features into account.

What we do

We install, repair and renew standing seam and lock seam roofs. We make sure the roof is watertight, durable and tidy, and that the result serves for years.

Who it suits

A standing seam roof suits detached, semi-detached and terraced houses, apartment buildings as well as office and commercial buildings.

Process

How the work proceeds

1. Contact and survey

You get in touch and tell us what needs to be done.

2. Site inspection

We inspect the site, assess the condition of the roof or facade and clarify your wishes and the scope of the work.

3. Solution and quote

We propose the best technical solution and draw up a clear quote.

4. Agreement and preparation

We agree on materials, the details of the work and the schedule, after which the work can begin.

5. Carrying out the work

We do the work professionally and carefully, on the agreed schedule and to the agreed quality.

6. Inspection and handover

We inspect the result, clean up the work area and hand over the finished site.
